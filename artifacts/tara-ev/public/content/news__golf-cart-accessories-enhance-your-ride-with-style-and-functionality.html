Medium Speed Vehicle Accessories: Enhance Your Ride with Style and Functionality

Explore the top medium speed vehicle accessories that turn a basic ride into a customized experience, improving comfort, safety, and usability.

1. What accessories can I put on my medium speed vehicle?

When upgrading your medium speed vehicle, a wide range of accessories is available:

Windshields come in fold-down, tinted, or full-size versions, improving safety and weather protection.

Lift kits and larger tires tackle rough terrain and add a sporty look.

Seats and storage options — from plush seat covers to under-seat storage bins or rear cargo boxes.

Lights, mirrors, and sound systems convert your cart into a road-worthy golf car.

On the TARA Medium Speed Vehicles site, you‘ll find factory-installed packages, ensuring compatibility and easy installation.

2. Are medium speed vehicle accessories worth it?

Investing in accessories depends on usage and goals:

For daily neighborhood rides, mirrors, seat belts, and LED lighting enhance safety and compliance.

For resort or park use, comfort upgrades like upgraded seats, stereo systems, and sun canopies boost user experience.

For golf enthusiasts, functional items—like club holders, cooler baskets, scorecard holders—provide real convenience.

While accessories add cost, they pay off through usability, enjoyment, and the potential to increase resale value. Premium carts like Tara‘s Spirit Plus or Roadster 2+2 come ready-equipped and hold their value better.

3. How do I accessorize my medium speed vehicle?

A smart accessory plan follows these steps:

Identify your needs – commute, leisure, family, or course use.

Pick categories – safety, comfort, performance, aesthetics.

Choose quality over quantity – invest in durable, branded items.

Match accessories to your cart – ensure fit for model and powertrain; lithium carts need compatible electronics.

4. Do I need a windshield on my medium speed vehicle?

Yes, especially if you ride in public or during inclement weather:

Fold-down windshields offer convenience and minimal obstruction.

Full windshields block wind, dust, and rain for improved safety and comfort.

Tinted or tinted top models protect passengers from glare and UV rays.

Tara‘s accessory catalog includes windshields tailored for each model, ensuring perfect alignment and no stress on mounting hardware.

Conclusion

Before buying accessories, define how you use your cart—whether for golf, neighborhood rides, family outings, or resort service. Choose durable, model-specific products that match your cart‘s powertrain, especially for lithium-medium speed vehicles. With the right mix, your cart becomes more comfortable, safe, and stylish—not to mention potentially increasing resale value.
