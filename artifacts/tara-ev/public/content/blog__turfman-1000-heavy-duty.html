Turfman 1000: Heavy-Duty Electric Utility

The Turfman 1000 is the largest member of the TARA utility family, built for the jobs that used to require a small truck: bulk material transport, irrigation repair runs, and event logistics.

Electric power means no fuel storage, no exhaust in enclosed areas, and dramatically lower running costs than combustion utility vehicles.
