Reflecting on 2024: A Transformative Year for the Medium Speed Vehicle Industry and What to Expect in 2025

TARA Medium Speed Vehicles wishes all our valued customers and partners a very Merry Christmas and a Happy New Year! May the holiday season bring you joy, peace, and exciting new opportunities in the year ahead.


As 2024 draws to a close, the medium speed vehicle industry finds itself at a pivotal moment. From increased adoption of electric medium speed vehicles to evolving technologies and shifting consumer preferences, this year has proven to be a period of significant transformation. Looking ahead to 2025, the industry is poised to continue its growth, with sustainability, innovation, and increased nationwide demand at the forefront of developments.

2024: A Year of Growth and Sustainability

The medium speed vehicle market has seen a steady rise in demand throughout 2024, driven by the continued nationwide shift toward medium speed vehicles (EVs) and greater emphasis on environmental sustainability. Sustainability remains a key driver, with 76% of golf courses across the United States opting to replace traditional gasoline-powered carts with electric alternatives by 2024, according to data from the National Golf Foundation (NGF). Not only do electric medium speed vehicles offer reduced emissions, but they also provide lower operational costs over time due to the reduced need for maintenance compared to gas-powered models.

Technological Advancements: Enhancing the Golfing Experience

Technology continues to play a central role in the development of modern medium speed vehicles. In 2024, advanced features like GPS integration, fleet management system, and real-time performance tracking have become standard in many high-end models. Additionally, driverless medium speed vehicles and autonomous systems are no longer just concepts—they are being tested at select golf courses across the United States.

TARA Medium Speed Vehicles has embraced these advancements, with its fleet of carts now featuring smart connectivity and advanced suspension systems that enhance comfort and performance. Moreover, new additions to their models include fleet management system for course managers to track battery life, maintenance schedules, and cart usage.

Looking Ahead to 2025: Continued Growth and Innovation

As we move into 2025, the medium speed vehicle industry is expected to continue its upward trajectory. The U.S. market for electric medium speed vehicles continues to grow steadily, according to Allied Market Research, as more golf courses and resorts invest in eco-friendly fleets and new technology.

Sustainability will remain a central theme, with golf courses increasingly adopting renewable energy sources like solar-powered charging stations to further reduce their environmental footprint. By 2025, experts predict that over 50% of golf courses across the United States will incorporate solar charging solutions for their electric cart fleets, marking a significant step toward making the golf industry more environmentally responsible.

In terms of innovation, GPS integration and advanced course management systems are likely to become more mainstream by 2025. These technologies promise to enhance course operations by offering features like map navigation and real-time tracking, which not only streamline fleet management but also enable golf courses to stay in constant communication with players through the fleet management system, making it easier to respond promptly to customer needs and improve the overall experience.

TARA Medium Speed Vehicles is also poised to expand its reach across the United States in 2025, especially in the South and Southwest, which are projected to become major growth regions.

Conclusion: The Path Ahead

2024 has been a year of significant progress for the medium speed vehicle industry, with sustainable solutions, technological innovation, and strong market growth at the forefront. As we look ahead to 2025, the medium speed vehicle market is expected to evolve even further, driven by increased demand for electric carts, smarter technologies, and a continued focus on reducing the environmental impact of the sport.

For golf course owners, managers, and players alike, the next year promises to bring exciting opportunities to enhance the golfing experience while contributing to a greener planet.
