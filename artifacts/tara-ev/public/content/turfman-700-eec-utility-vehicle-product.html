Turfman 700 with EEC certification. This vehicle meets the EU regulations on safety, environmental protection, noise and other aspects, and can be legally driven on the road in Europe. The Turfman 700 EEC is equipped with a large-capacity cargo box to meet the needs of various working conditions such as golf course maintenance, green maintenance and logistics supply.

Vehicle Highlights

MULTIFUNCTION SWITCH

The multifunction switch integrates controls for the wiper, turn signals, headlights and other functions. You can complete the operation with just a flick of your finger, which is convenient.

CARGO BOX

The cargo box is made of durable material, which can easily carry all kinds of tools and materials, and is widely used in golf courses, farms and other work sites. The innovative lifting structure design saves time and effort in the unloading process.

LED LIGHTS

Our vehicles come standard with high-efficiency LED lighting that consumes less power and is brighter, with a wider illumination range than similar products, fully ensuring visibility and safety during nighttime driving.

TOWING HOOK

The towing hook delivers unmatched strength and lasting durability, capable of hauling lawn care equipment. No need for third-party towing services, the towing task can be completed easily.

TIRE

Tires that meet EEC certification requirements have been strictly designed and verified in terms of labeling, regulatory compliance, performance indicators and field adaptability, and can provide vehicles with a reliable and efficient driving experience.

ONE-PIECE WINDSHIELD

The high-quality one-piece windshield is equipped with a wiper, which ensures that the driving view is not blocked even in windy and rainy conditions, further improving safety.
