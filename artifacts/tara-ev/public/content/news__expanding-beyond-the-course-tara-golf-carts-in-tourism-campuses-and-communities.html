Electric Medium Speed Vehicles Beyond the Golf Course

Why are more and more non-golf scenarios choosing Tara as a green travel solution?

Tara medium speed vehicles have won wide acclaim on golf courses for their excellent performance and high-end design. But in fact, their value goes far beyond the fairways. Today, more and more tourist attractions, resorts, university campuses, communities and parks are choosing Tara as their green travel solution for the “last mile” and internal commuting in the park.

Tourism and High-End Resort Industry: Creating a Quiet and Comfortable Mobile Experience for Guests

In high-end resort hotels, island scenic spots and ecological parks, Tara medium speed vehicles are gradually replacing traditional fuel shuttles. Tara offers a variety of models from 2 to 4 seats, equipped with a silent drive system and lithium battery power, which not only improves the reception level, but also ensures that guests experience a quieter, smoother and more environmentally friendly ride experience during the journey.

The exterior design of the vehicle is more modern, and the body color, logo and interior can also be customized according to the resort’s visual system to enhance brand unity. With a lightweight body and flexible steering system, it can easily pass even in narrow park sections or crowded areas.

Campus and Large Venues: Providing Low-Carbon Support for Efficient Operations

In large facilities such as university campuses, exhibition centers, and science and technology parks, Tara multi-purpose medium speed vehicles are widely used for internal transportation between teaching buildings, office areas, event venues, and other areas. The Tara fleet can be used for:

On-campus teacher and student transfers and visitor reception

Security patrols and logistics transportation

Staff dispatch at exhibitions and large events

All models are equipped with zero-emission lithium-ion power systems, which effectively reduce operating costs while supporting all-weather operation. Thanks to its compact body and quiet driving characteristics, the vehicle can flexibly shuttle between crowded or restricted areas to improve management efficiency.

Communities and Landscape Gardens: Achieving Green, Quiet, and Sustainable Daily Travel

In gated communities, health towns, urban parks, and landscape gardens, Tara small medium speed vehicles are becoming an ideal choice for residents’ daily short-distance travel and property management. Its advantages are:

Zero noise, no disturbance to surrounding residents or tourists

Zero emissions, protect air quality and natural environment

Simple and easy to use, elderly can also ride with peace of mind

Full-Scenario Product Portfolio to Meet Different Use Needs

Tara provides a diversified product line, including: golf series, utility vehicles, and personal series. Each model supports multiple customized configurations, from battery capacity, number of seats to accessories selection, to help customers build an exclusive green transportation system that meets their own operational needs.

Shaping a More Sustainable Mobile Ecology

Tara always adheres to the core concept of “green drive, elegant travel” and continues to optimize electrification solutions. Whether on the golf course, or in tourism, campus, community and other scenarios, Tara is committed to promoting the popularization and upgrading of green travel with high-quality products and services.
