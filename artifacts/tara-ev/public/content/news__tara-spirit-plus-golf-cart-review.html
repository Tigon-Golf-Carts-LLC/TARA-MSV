Tara Spirit Plus Medium Speed Vehicle Review

When purchasing high-spec two-seater medium speed vehicles, courses typically prioritize aesthetics, power, comfort, and fleet management capabilities. Positioned as a premium electric two-seater for modern facilities, the Tara Spirit Plus is ideal for private member clubs, resort-style courses, and operations looking to upgrade their vehicle specifications.

Powertrain Configuration

The Tara Spirit Plus features a lithium battery system, a 48V 6.3kW AC motor, and a 400A controller, delivering an official top speed of 20 km/h. Compared to the Spirit Pro (which uses a 48V 4kW AC motor), the Spirit Plus offers higher power output, making it well-suited for courses with steep inclines, frequent stop-and-go driving, or high-intensity usage.

As a lithium-powered cart, it eliminates the need for the regular water refilling required by traditional lead-acid batteries, simplifying daily charging logistics for the course. However, actual range is influenced by factors such as terrain, payload, driving habits, and ambient temperature; battery configuration should be selected based on specific course conditions.

Riding Experience

This two-seater cart is equipped with luxury seating, an adjustable steering wheel, a multi-functional dashboard, USB charging ports, and a folding windshield. It rides on 12-inch aluminum alloy wheels paired with radial tires, balancing water drainage, traction, and steering stability.

The rear of the vehicle features a golf bag rack and a storage basket, with optional accessories including an ice bucket, sand bottle, ball washer, and rain cover. Unlike models designed solely for basic transport, the Tara Spirit Plus prioritizes passenger comfort and convenient storage throughout a round of golf.

Onboard Features

The Spirit Plus comes equipped with a 9-inch touchscreen, an illuminated audio system, and an optional built-in refrigerator. While these features may not be essential for every course, they add significant value for clubs that prioritize member experience, tournament hosting, and a resort-style atmosphere.

For large fleets, the vehicle offers optional integration with Tara’s GPS system. This allows for monitoring of vehicle location, battery State of Charge (SoC), speed, fault codes, and usage history, while supporting advanced functions such as geofencing, remote locking, and “Pace of Play” monitoring.

Target Courses

If a club requires only basic player transportation, a model with a more streamlined configuration might better suit the budget. If a golf course requires greater power, luxury seating, an onboard touchscreen, and GPS management capabilities, the Tara Spirit Plus Medium Speed Vehicle is an ideal choice.

TARA Medium Speed Vehicles offers two-seater medium speed vehicles, four-seater models, and the Turfman series of utility vehicles. TARA Medium Speed Vehicles allows for a combination of the Spirit Plus and other models to meet diverse needs, such as on-course play, member transport, and maintenance operations.

FAQs

Is the Tara Spirit Plus Medium Speed Vehicle electric?

Yes. It is an electric medium speed vehicle powered by a lithium battery system and an AC motor.

How many seats does the Spirit Plus have?

The standard model is equipped with two luxury seats, making it a two-seater medium speed vehicle.

What is the top speed of the Tara Spirit Plus?

Official specifications indicate a top speed of 20 km/h. Actual speed and performance may vary depending on load, terrain (slope), and operating conditions.

Does the Spirit Plus support GPS?

It supports an optional Tara GPS system, which enables vehicle tracking, geofencing, battery monitoring, fault diagnostics, and fleet operation analysis.

What is the difference between the Spirit Plus and the Spirit Pro?

The Spirit Plus features a 6.3 kW AC motor and 12-inch aluminum alloy wheels, offering a wider range of comfort and smart features; the Spirit Pro utilizes a 4 kW AC motor and a more basic fleet-oriented configuration. They are positioned differently in the market.
